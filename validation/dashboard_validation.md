- More differentiated products have larger distances
- In the simulation, removed products are matched to the closest remaining substitute
- Example: removing Apple iPhone 11 returned Apple iPhone 11 (128GB) as closest substitute with a low distance

### Questionnaire page (Module 2, Part B-1)

| Check | Expected | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Build spec from `conjoint_demo.xlsx` | 7 active B-1 rating attributes, scale 1–5 | ✅ |
| Submit ratings → `responses.csv` | contract-compliant frame, passes `validate_responses` | ✅ |
| Feed into `python -m conjoint.estimate` | writes a 7-attribute `importance.csv` | ✅ (small-sample RI ≠ N=381 by design — see note) |

> **Caveat:** estimating from a handful of live responses does **not** reproduce
> the published N=381 percentages — small samples are noisy. Algorithm
> correctness is proven by the N=381 fixture tests
> (`test_n381_importance_within_tolerance`); the questionnaire page demonstrates
> the *collection → estimation* plumbing, not a re-run of the 381-respondent study.
